# LLM discoverability

This work complements traditional SEO. llms.txt is a proposed convention (https://llmstxt.org/); it is not a ranking factor and no AI system guarantees that it will read, cite or rank the site because of it.

## llms.txt

Served at `/llms.txt` by `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts`, generated by `generateLlmsTxt()` in `src/lib/llms/generate.ts` from the same registries that render the site. Structure follows the proposal: one H1 (`# MAB Global Partners`), a blockquote summary, a short prose section (identity, focus markets, sister brand, contact details, scope note) and H2 link lists:

* `## Company`: homepage, about, contact
* `## Services`: services index and every published service
* `## Industries`: industries index and every published industry
* `## Insights`: insights index and every published guide
* `## Optional`: llms-full.txt, legal notice, privacy policy, KVKK notice

All links are absolute canonical URLs. A unit test checks that every link is a published sitemap URL, and an end-to-end test requests each one.

## llms-full.txt

Served at `/llms-full.txt`. Justification: service and sector pages are long, multi-section HTML documents, and a plain-text consolidation of definitions, scope, process, considerations and FAQs gives answer engines a clean, unambiguous representation of what the company actually offers. It does not duplicate legal pages or navigation. It is regenerated on every build, so it cannot drift from the pages.

## Page content for retrieval

Each service page states, in its own section: what the service is (one-sentence definition), which jurisdiction applies, who it is for, the scope, the process, documents that may be needed, factors that need individual assessment and how to get in touch. Educational guides live in Bilgi Merkezi, cite official sources (IRS, SBA, GOV.UK, Companies House) and carry a "general information, not personal advice" notice. Company capabilities and general information are kept in separate page types.

## AI crawler policy

Reviewed against operator documentation on 2026-10-12:

| Agent | Operator | Purpose | robots.txt |
|---|---|---|---|
| OAI-SearchBot | OpenAI | Surfaces sites in ChatGPT search | Honored, controls search inclusion |
| ChatGPT-User | OpenAI | User-initiated visits | May not apply |
| GPTBot | OpenAI | Model training | Honored |
| Claude-SearchBot | Anthropic | Search quality and indexing | Honored |
| Claude-User | Anthropic | Fetches pages for user questions | Honored |
| ClaudeBot | Anthropic | Model training | Honored |
| PerplexityBot | Perplexity | Search results, states it is not used for training | Recommended to allow |
| Perplexity-User | Perplexity | User-requested fetches | Generally ignores robots.txt |
| Google-Extended | Google | Control token for Gemini training and grounding | Does not affect Google Search inclusion or ranking |
| Applebot-Extended | Apple | Control token for model training | Honored |
| CCBot | Common Crawl | Open crawl used by many models | Honored |

Current decision: allow search, user-initiated and training agents on public marketing pages, because the business goal is discoverability and all published content is public. `/api/` is blocked for every agent. **This is pending business confirmation.** To opt out of training while keeping search visibility, set `ALLOW_AI_TRAINING_CRAWLERS=false` and redeploy. Agents that act on a user's behalf may ignore robots.txt by design; private data must never rely on robots.txt for protection (the site has no private areas).

## Entity consistency

Name, canonical URL, email, address and maps link come from `src/config/site.ts` and `src/config/contact.ts` and are used unchanged in the footer, contact page, structured data, llms.txt and llms-full.txt. The about page and llms-full.txt share the same paragraphs from `src/content/about.ts`.

## Maintenance

* Publishing, updating or unpublishing a service, sector or guide updates llms.txt, llms-full.txt and the sitemap automatically on the next build.
* Review this crawler table every six months; operators add and rename agents.
* After launch, check how the brand is represented in major answer engines and correct the source pages, not the llms files.
